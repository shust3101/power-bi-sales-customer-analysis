# DAX Measures

This file documents the custom DAX measures created for the Power BI Sales & Customer Analysis Dashboard.

The measures were used to calculate sales performance, customer activity, average values, income metrics, and year-over-year changes.

## Sales Measures

### Total Sales

Calculates the total sales value in the selected filter context.

### Average Order Value

Calculates the average value per order.

### Average Sales per Customer

Calculates average sales generated per customer.

### Avg Sales per Active Customer

Calculates average sales generated only by customers who made purchases in the selected period.

### Customer Total Sales

Calculates total sales for each customer.

## Customer Measures

### Total Customers

Calculates the total number of customers in the dataset.

### Unique Customers

Calculates the number of unique customers.

### Total Unique Customers All Time

Calculates the total number of unique customers across the full dataset.

### Customers This Period

Calculates the number of customers with sales in the selected period.

### % Customers Active

Calculates the percentage of active customers in the selected period compared to all customers.

### HasSales

Identifies whether a customer has sales in the selected filter context.

## Income Measure

### Median Yearly Income

Calculates the median yearly income of customers.

## Year-over-Year Measures

### YoY Sales

Calculates the difference in sales compared to the previous year.

### YoY Sales Growth

Calculates the percentage change in sales compared to the previous year.
